Laptops and Hardware

Standard laptops are replaced every 4 years. Early replacement is possible if the laptop is damaged or cannot run required software; your manager approves the request.

If a laptop is damaged, report it within 2 business days and include how it happened. Liquid damage should be reported immediately; switch the laptop off and do not charge it.

Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the service desk and security team within 24 hours so the device can be locked and wiped remotely.

Accessories (monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset) are ordered through the Company Portal and normally arrive within 5 business days. Docking stations are only provided for desk-based staff.
