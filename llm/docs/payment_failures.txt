Payment Failure Troubleshooting

If customer payments fail:
- check the payment gateway status page for an ongoing incident
- check the payment service logs for the error code
- confirm the customer's billing details and card expiry date
- ask the customer to retry after 5 minutes

Error code PG-402 means the card was declined by the bank; the customer must contact their bank. PG-504 means the gateway timed out; the payment was not taken and it is safe to retry.

If more than 5% of payments fail within 15 minutes, treat it as a Sev1 incident and page the on-call engineer. Never ask customers to send full card numbers by email or chat.
