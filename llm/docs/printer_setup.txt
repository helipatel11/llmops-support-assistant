Printing

Office printers use secure "follow-me" printing. Send the job to the "FollowMe" queue, then tap your badge on any office printer to release it. Jobs not released within 8 hours are deleted.

To add the FollowMe queue on a company laptop, open the Company Portal app and install "FollowMe Printing". You must be on the Corp network or the VPN.

If the printer says "badge not recognised", register your badge once by entering your employee ID on the printer screen.

Paper jams and empty toner should be reported using the QR code on the printer, not through a service desk ticket. Colour printing is disabled by default; managers can request it for their team.
